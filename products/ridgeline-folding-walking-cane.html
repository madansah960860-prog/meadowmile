An aluminium cane that folds into four sections on an internal elastic, so it goes into a daypack rather than being carried. The height adjusts through eight positions from 33 to 37 inches with a push-button collar, and the handle is a moulded offset grip rather than a crook. A wrist strap and a fabric sleeve are included.
