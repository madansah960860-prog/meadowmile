Refund & Return Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

If something is not right, send it back. You have 30 days from the day it is delivered, and the process below takes about five minutes to start.

The return window

You may return an item within 30 days of delivery. The clock starts on the day the carrier records the parcel as delivered, and it is the date you tell us you want to return that counts — not the date the parcel reaches us. Contact us inside the 30 days and you are inside the window, even if the parcel takes another week to come back.

Please try everything indoors first, and do try it properly: put the vest on over your coat, load the pack on the carpet, set the poles to height and lean on them on a hard floor. What we cannot take back is kit that has been outside on a wet path, and the reason is below.

Condition we need it in

  • Unused outdoors — tried indoors is fine, walked on a wet path is not.
  • In its original packaging, with the sleeve, straps, tips or baskets still included.
  • Complete — everything listed under “what is in the box” on the product page.
  • Undamaged by use. Ordinary opening of the box is expected; we do not mind an opened carton.

If an item comes back short of this, we will contact you before doing anything. Where the item is still saleable at a lower value we may offer a partial refund, with the reason and the figure stated, and you can tell us to send it back to you instead at no charge.

Non-returnable items

This list is short and we do not add to it quietly. The following cannot be returned unless they are faulty, damaged in transit, or not what you ordered:

  • Anything worn or used outdoors, the reflective vest, the rain jacket and the daypack included. Try them on indoors, walk around the house, load the pack on the carpet — all fine. Once something has been out on a wet path we cannot sell it as new.
  • The cane and the poles once the tips have touched the ground outside, because the rubber ferrule marks immediately. Adjust the height indoors and lean on it on a hard floor first; that does not affect your return.
  • The water bottle once it has been filled, for hygiene reasons. Check the lid action and the grip dry.

Everything else in the shop can be returned. Nothing is excluded because it was reduced, because it was the last one, or because you have returned something before.

How to return something

  1. Tell us. Email support@meadowmile.com with “Return” and your order number in the subject line, or call (208) 555-0126 during Mon–Fri, 11:00 AM–7:00 PM ET. Say what you are sending back and why. “Changed my mind” is a complete answer and we will not press you further.
  2. We send you a return number. Within one business day you will get a return authorisation number and the address to send to, in writing.
  3. Pack it. Use the original packaging if you still have it, or any box that protects the item. Write the return number clearly on the outside and put a note with it inside.
  4. Send it to the returns address, which is the same as our mailing address:

    Meadowmile Outfitters LLC
    Returns — quote your return number
    452 Cedar Ridge Parkway, Suite 7
    Boise, ID 83702

    Please use a tracked service. Until it reaches us it is still your parcel, and a tracking number is the only way to prove where it got to.
  5. We inspect and refund. See the timings below.

Damaged, faulty or wrong items

Tell us within 7 days of delivery if a parcel arrives damaged or the contents are broken, so we can make a claim with the carrier while the evidence is fresh. Email support@meadowmile.com with:

  • Your order number.
  • A photograph of the damage.
  • A photograph of the outer box, including the label.

We will send a replacement or refund you in full — your choice — and we pay the return postage. If you would rather keep a slightly marked item at a reduced price, say so and we will agree a figure with you. We will not ask you to deal with the carrier yourself.

If a fault appears later, contact us anyway. Outside the 30 days we are no longer obliged to take it back, but we would rather hear about it than not, and in practice we usually help.

Refunds

Method. We refund to the payment method you used. We do not issue store credit unless you ask for it.

Timing. We inspect returns within 2 business days of the parcel arriving, and issue the refund within 5–10 business days of that inspection. We email you when the refund is sent. Your bank or card issuer may then take a few more days to show it on your statement, which is out of our hands.

Where the Federal Trade Commission’s timings are shorter, they apply. If you paid by cash, cheque or money order, we refund within seven working days of accepting the return. If you paid on a store credit account, we credit it within one billing cycle. If we cancel an order before shipping it, the refund is issued immediately and always within seven working days.

Worn ferrules, tips and baskets

These are consumable parts and they are not a return. Rubber ferrules on the cane and poles wear out — that is what they are for. Email support@meadowmile.com and we will post replacement ferrules, carbide tips or baskets at cost, whenever you need them. Do not keep walking on a worn ferrule.

Exchanges

We do not run a formal exchange process, because in practice it is slower for you. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the one you want — that way the new item ships straight away instead of waiting for the old one to arrive. If you would rather we held the new item for you, call and we will.

Cancelling before it ships

If you change your mind before the parcel is handed to the carrier, email support@meadowmile.com or call (208) 555-0126 and we will cancel it and refund you in full. Since we ship within 2–3 business days, please do get in touch quickly.

If a delivery is late or does not arrive

That is handled in the Shipping Policy, which sets out what we do if we cannot meet the shipping time, and how lost parcels are traced. In short: you can always cancel for a full refund rather than wait.
